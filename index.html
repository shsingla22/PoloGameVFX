VICTORY

Desktop

  • W / S — change gait (halt · walk · trot · canter · gallop)
  • A / D — steer   Shift — flat-out (uses stamina)
  • Hold Space / Left mouse — wind up, release to strike a forehand
  • F / Right mouse — backhand   Q — switch off-side / near-side
  • Mouse — fine aim (click to lock)   C — camera   M — mute   Esc — pause
  • Gamepad: left stick ride, RT forehand, LT backhand, A sprint, LB side

Meta Quest

  • Right controller is your mallet — swing for real; harder swings hit further
  • Left stick — push up/down to change gait, left/right to steer
  • Grip / trigger — gallop flat out   B or Y — recentre seat height   A — aim assist

Rules that matter

  • Keep the ball on your off-side (right) for the strongest shots.
  • The rider following the line of the ball has right of way. Crossing in front of them is a foul.
  • Side-by-side ride-offs are legal; T-boning at speed is dangerous riding.
  • Teams change ends after every goal. A tie after the last chukker goes to a golden-goal extra chukker.
